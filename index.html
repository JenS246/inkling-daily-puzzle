    How to play

    1. Every word in the cloud belongs to at least one hidden phrase.
    2. Bigger words appear in more phrases. A word fades only after every phrase using it is found.
    3. Type a complete phrase and press Enter. Wrong guesses become splatters in your Inkprint, but they never end the game.

    Color is Today's Ink. It changes the look of the puzzle only. Color never shows how many guesses you used and never affects play.

    You have three hints. They reveal the phrase type, its first word, then outline its words in the cloud. The revealed hint appears above your guess box.
